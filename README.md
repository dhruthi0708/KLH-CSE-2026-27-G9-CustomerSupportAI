
<h1 align="center">Prompting vs RAG vs Fine-Tuning for Customer Support Automation</h1>

<h2>1. Project Overview</h2>

<p>
This project focuses on exploring and comparing three Generative AI techniques: Prompt Engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Fine-Tuning for customer support automation.
</p>

<p>
The aim is to develop an AI-powered customer support chatbot that can understand customer queries and generate relevant responses using these three approaches.
</p>

<p>
The project evaluates their performance based on response accuracy, relevance, response time, reliability, and implementation cost to identify the most suitable approach for automating customer support services.
</p>

<h2>2. Project Objectives</h2>

<ol>
  <li>Explore the role of Generative AI in customer support automation.</li>
  <li>Implement Prompt Engineering to generate customer support responses.</li>
  <li>Develop a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system using a knowledge base.</li>
  <li>Explore Fine-Tuning of pre-trained language models for customer support.</li>
  <li>Compare the three approaches based on their performance.</li>
  <li>Identify the strengths and limitations of each technique.</li>
</ol>

<h2>6. Project Architecture</h2>

<p>The proposed system consists of three major approaches:</p>

<p><strong>1. Prompt Engineering</strong></p>
<p>
Uses carefully designed instructions to guide an AI model in generating appropriate customer support responses.
</p>

<p><strong>2. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)</strong></p>
<p>
Retrieves relevant information from a customer support knowledge base, such as FAQs and company policies, before generating responses.
</p>

<p><strong>3. Fine-Tuning</strong></p>
<p>
Adapts a pre-trained language model using customer support examples to improve its task-specific behaviour and response style.
</p>

<p>
The outputs generated by these approaches will be evaluated and compared using common customer queries.
</p>

<h2>7. Expected Outcome</h2>

<p>
The expected outcome is an AI-powered customer support automation system that demonstrates and compares Prompt Engineering, RAG, and Fine-Tuning.
</p>

<p>
The project aims to identify how these techniques can improve customer query handling, response quality, and the overall efficiency of customer support services.
</p>
